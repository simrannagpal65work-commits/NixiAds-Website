Scaling Your Catalog: AI Photos for Amazon, Shopify & Beyond

9 min read·E-commerce Operations

You're selling on three platforms: your own Shopify store, Amazon, and TikTok Shop. Each platform has different photo requirements. Different layouts. Different audience expectations. Different conversion drivers.

Building three separate content sets for the same product is exhausting. And expensive. Traditional approach: hire a photographer, do one shoot, adapt photos for each platform. Reality? You end up with one decent photo set and compromise everywhere else.

AI changes this. You generate one master set of product photos, then create platform-specific variants automatically.

The Platform Photo Requirements Problem

Amazon

  • First image must be pure white background (hard requirement)
  • Shows product 85%+ of frame
  • Multiple angles (front, back, side, detail)
  • Lifestyle shots perform 15-20% better but aren't required
  • Infographics (size comparison, material closeup) essential

Shopify (DTC)

  • Lifestyle shots convert better than product-only
  • On-model imagery drives higher AOV
  • Room/scene context builds brand aspiration
  • Hero image (first shown) is crucial
  • Flexibility with ratios and styling

TikTok Shop / Instagram

  • Vertical video preferred (9:16 aspect)
  • Carousel format (multiple products in one shot)
  • Lifestyle + product + close-up combination
  • Aspirational styling (not just product shots)
  • Trending aesthetics matter (changes monthly)

Building unique photos for each channel from scratch: $5,000-15,000 per product and 6-12 weeks.

With AI: Generate one master set ($500-2,000 total), create platform-specific variants ($0, just recropping/restyling).

The Master Set Strategy

What goes in your master catalog:

  1. White background product shots (3-5 angles: front, back, side, detail, scale)
  2. Lifestyle on-model shots (2-3 different contexts)
  3. Styled flatlay/hero shots
  4. Close-up detail shots (material, craftsmanship, label)
  5. Size comparison shots (product next to reference object)

This is 15-20 images per product. Generated from your product photo in about 2-3 hours of prompt refinement and variant selection.

Then Create Platform-Specific Variants

Amazon: Use white backgrounds + detail shots + size comparison. Skip lifestyle. Emphasize angles and clarity.

Shopify: Lead with lifestyle. Feature hero lifestyle shot first. Include on-model variants. Use aspirational styling. (E.g., luxury watch = minimalist white room + professional styling vs. outdoor adventure context for sports watch).

TikTok/Instagram: Create 9:16 crops of best lifestyle shots. Layer text overlay ("Free shipping" / trending audio hooks). Add product detail shots. Think scroll-stopping, not professional.

Each platform gets 5-8 optimized images from your 20-image master set. No new shoot required.

Scaling This to 100+ Products

For a brand with 100 SKUs:

Traditional approach: 5-6 shoots per year across categories. Coverage: 30-40% of catalog. Cost per SKU: $60-150. Time to new images: 6-8 weeks.

AI approach: Generate variants on-demand. Coverage: 100% of catalog. Cost per SKU: $15-30. Time to variants: 1-2 hours.

The time and cost savings allow you to:

  • Refresh seasonal photos monthly (trending aesthetics, seasonal styling)
  • A/B test photos across platforms (discover which image styles convert best per platform)
  • Support inventory changes instantly (new color launches get lifestyle photos same day)
  • Iterate on aesthetics (customer feedback on styling? Generate variants with different aesthetic instantly)

The Workflow: From Master Set to Platform Live

Day 1: Generate master set (upload product photo → specify model persona, scenes, angles → generate 20 variants → curate best 15-18 images)

Day 2-3: Create platform-specific variants (crop to platform specs → adjust color grading if needed → add overlays for social → upload to platform)

Result: One product, three platforms, uniquely optimized photos, in 2-3 days vs. 6-8 weeks.

What Works Across All Platforms (Universal Wins)

  • Clear product visibility (30%+ of frame minimum)
  • Lifestyle context beats pure product shots (15-25% conversion lift)
  • Size/scale reference (hand holding product, comparison object) matters
  • Color consistency with your brand identity (matters more than objectively "pretty" colors)

Common Multi-Channel Mistakes

Mistake 1: Using identical photos across all platforms. Each platform has different expectations and conversion drivers. Optimize per platform.

Mistake 2: Over-optimizing for Amazon white background. Amazon is important but your Shopify store where you have direct customer relationships matters more for LTV. Invest more in Shopify lifestyle content.

Mistake 3: Not testing which image styles convert best per platform. Generate 3-5 style variants, deploy them, measure which converts best. Then that becomes your template for similar products.
