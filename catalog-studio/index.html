Common questions

Everything you need to know about AI product photography with Nixi.

What is AI product photography and how does it work?

AI product photography uses generative AI to create professional product photos without a physical camera, studio, or photographer. With Nixi's Catalog Studio, you upload one flatlay or product image, choose a model, scene, and pose, and the AI generates studio-quality lifestyle and catalog shots in minutes.

Can I create product photos without a professional photoshoot?

Yes. Catalog Studio lets you generate product photos without a photographer, studio, or model booking. Upload a single product image and Nixi's AI produces multiple studio-quality shots - ready for your website, Amazon, Shopify, or Instagram - in hours instead of weeks.

How do AI-generated model photos work for product images?

Nixi lets you choose from a library of real AI models - different body types, ethnicities, and looks - to showcase your product. The AI blends your actual product with the selected model and scene, producing realistic on-model photos without hiring a model or renting a studio.
