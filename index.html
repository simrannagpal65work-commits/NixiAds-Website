Common questions

Everything you need to know about creating ads and product photos with Nixi.

What is Nixi and what can I create with it?

Nixi is an AI creative engine with two studios: Ad Studio for generating scroll-stopping ad creatives for Meta and Instagram, and Catalog Studio for AI product photoshoots and videos with real-looking models. You can create ads, product photos, catalog videos, and clone existing ad styles - all without a camera, studio, or design team.

Do I need design or photography experience to use Nixi?

No. Nixi is built for founders and marketers, not designers. In Ad Studio, paste your product URL and pick a campaign angle, and the AI generates the full ad creative for you. In Catalog Studio, upload a product image and choose a model, pose, and scene, and the AI generates the photoshoot. No editing software or shoot required either way.

How is Nixi different from hiring a photographer or designer?

A traditional shoot or design project takes days or weeks and costs significantly more per asset. Nixi generates studio-quality ad creatives and product photos in minutes using credits, so you can test more variations and refresh your catalog far faster and cheaper.
